International Jury

Festival Jury

The international jury brings together filmmakers, producers and cultural leaders from BRICS+ countries and partner states.

🇧🇷 Brazil

Antonio Miano

Actor, Director, Curator

Brazilian actor and director working across cinema, theatre, and performance art. Director of short and documentary films. Curator of film programs and audience development initiatives.

🇧🇷 BRAZIL

Padu Palmério

Director, Cinematographer

Brazilian director and cinematographer working in both fiction and documentary cinema. Has filmed projects in more than 40 countries and collaborated on international productions across Latin America. Recipient and nominee of international cinematography awards, and an active jury member and mentor in international film laboratories.

🇨🇺 Cuba

Alejandro Gil Álvarez

Director, Screenwriter

Cuban film director and screenwriter, recipient of national cultural awards and international film festival honors. Author of documentary and fiction films, television projects, and music videos. Jury member of major film festivals across Latin America and Europe.

🇮🇷 Iran

Babak Khajehpasha

Director, Screenwriter

Iranian director and screenwriter, creator of feature films and theatre productions. His film In the Arms of the Tree was selected as Iran’s submission for the 2025 Academy Awards. Recipient of international awards at film festivals in the United States, India, Italy, and Canada, including honors for Best Director and Best Screenplay.

🇮🇳 India

Jayita Ghosh

International Cultural Relations Expert

Indian expert in cinema, international communications, and cultural diplomacy. Leads international initiatives aimed at strengthening cooperation between creative industries, business communities, and public institutions. Actively promotes cultural exchange and international film collaboration.

🇧🇾 Belarus

Vladimir Zinkevich

Film Director, Screenwriter

Belarusian film director and screenwriter, founder of the production company Avantdrive. Director of the acclaimed films Spice Boys and Welcome to the Family. Recipient of the Best Feature Film Award at the New Season Online Cinema Festival.

🇷🇺 Russia

Ivan Bolotnikov

Film Director, Screenwriter, Producer

Russian director, screenwriter, and producer, founder of Studio Avtor. Director of the films Kharms, Palmira, and Don’t Bury Me Without Ivan. Recipient of two Golden Goblet Awards at the Shanghai International Film Festival. A graduate of the Higher Courses for Scriptwriters and Directors, he worked in Aleksei German’s creative team on the film Hard to Be a God.

🇷🇺 Russia

Nikolai Izvolov

Film Historian, Cinema Scholar

PhD in Art History and one of the leading experts on early Russian and Soviet cinema. Author of major research works and restoration projects dedicated to lost films by Dziga Vertov, Lev Kuleshov, and Alexander Medvedkin. Lecturer at VGIK and the Higher Courses for Scriptwriters and Directors.

🇷🇺 RUSSIA

Alexey Kravchenko

International Journalist

International journalist and Chief Editor at the TASS Analytical Center. He specializes in international affairs, analysis, and coverage of global political and social processes.

🇷🇺 RUSSIA

Alexandra Ochirova

Doctor of Philosophy, Public Figure

Doctor of Philosophy and Full Member of the Russian Academy of Arts. UNESCO Goodwill Ambassador and Co-Chair of the General Council of the Assembly of the Peoples of the World. Co-President of the All-Russian Public Movement For the Preservation of the Nation.

Professionalism, independence and respect for cultural diversity are the guiding principles of the Festival Jury.
Official Conditions

Regulations

Main structure of the regulations. The full festival regulations are available as a PDF document.

  • 1
    Feature-length, short, documentary and popular science films from BRICS+ countries, as well as international co-productions, are eligible for participation.
  • 2
    Films completed on or after 1 January 2024 are eligible for participation
  • 3
    English subtitles are mandatory. Films in languages other than Russian must include both Russian and English subtitles.
  • 4
    Winners will receive the Grand Prix, Special Jury Awards and Special Festival Awards.

Main Award

Grand Prix

The highest award of the BRICS+ International Film Festival.
The Grand Prix and Special Jury Awards are granted by the International Jury.

The festival Grand Prix statuette is a symbol of the movement of BRICS+ cultures orbiting around the art of cinema and creating the energy of new stories.

The flame within the orbits is a metaphor for the creator whose work becomes the center of global attention.
