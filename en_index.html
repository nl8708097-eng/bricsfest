Festival Results

From 29 October to 3 November, cinema brought Moscow and Saint Petersburg together. On 6 November, the winners of the first BRICS+ International Film Festival were announced at a press conference at TASS.

The International Jury’s Decision

Festival Laureates

12 awards celebrating the art of storytelling, national character and cultural dialogue. Congratulations to the filmmakers, actors and film teams!

  1. 01

    Best Fiction Feature

    People’s Child

    Iran

    About the Film

    An orphaned teenager is forced to leave the orphanage. From his personal file, he learns that his family lost him, and he sets out to find them with hope. He does not yet know that this journey will become his path to growing up.

  2. 02

    Best Fiction Short

    Happan Saangwala

    India

    About the Film

    The film explores the vanishing folk art of swaang and the life of its master, Happan Saangi. His grandson Bittu helps him return to his craft and bring traditional performance together with modern technology.

  3. 03

    Best Documentary Feature

    Celestial Petersburg

    Russia

    About the Film

    A documentary about the cultural ties between Russia and China, explored through Saint Petersburg. Despite the distances and differences in their histories, the people in the film discover much in common in their outlook and attitudes to life.

    A cinematic journey through Saint Petersburg reveals how the city on the Neva has absorbed the best of world culture, art, architecture and science. Traces of Chinese culture emerge within its familiar European appearance.

    Chinese residents who live, work and study in Saint Petersburg, alongside local people passionate about China, help us see the city anew. Their stories reveal the strong bonds between the two peoples, their shared present and their outlook on the future.

  4. 04

    Best Documentary Short

    Three Sisters

    Russia

    About the Film

    A heartfelt story about family ties, the strength of the human spirit, traditions and harmony between people and nature.

  5. 05

    Special Festival Award

    For an Original Artistic Concept in a Fiction Short Film

    Ybi

    Brazil

    About the Film

    A film that explores the cultures of Indigenous peoples of the Amazon and environmental themes through fiction.

  6. 06

    Special Festival Award

    For a Vivid Interpretation of Historical Events

    Khodaye Jang

    God of War

    Iran

    About the Film

    Based on true events, the film tells the story of the development of Iran’s missile programme and the difficult path of its “father”, Hassan Tehrani-Moghaddam. The protagonist, Ibrahim Shariki, assembles a team of specialists tasked with urgently stopping the shelling of civilian cities and the loss of lives, with help from the Libyans.

    Khodaye Jang premiered at the 2025 Fajr International Film Festival, where it received the national Crystal Simorgh award.

  7. 07

    Special Festival Award

    Best Social Drama

    Calm Man

    Iran

    About the Film

    Nobahar, a young Afghan immigrant, is forced to marry an elderly man named Zaman. Eventually, she finds the resolve to take her destiny into her own hands.

  8. 08

    Special Festival Award

    For Thoughtful Treatment of Generational Memory

    Zero Meets Hero

    Vietnam

    About the Film

    A road story about a veteran and his Generation Z grandson as they search for his former comrades. A film about memory and the bonds between generations.

  9. 09

    Special Festival Award

    For Highlighting the Role of Science in Russian History

    Iskra

    Russia

    Director: Evgeny Karelinov

    About the Film

    The life of the outstanding Russian inventor and engineer Pavel Yablochkov, whose work revolutionised electrical engineering.

  10. 10

    Special Festival Award

    For Contributing to the Patriotic Education of the Younger Generation

    Seven Verst to Dawn

    Russia

    Director: Alexander Andreev

    About the Film

    Winter 1942, in the village of Kurakino. Amid the harsh conditions of the occupied Pskov region, Matvey Kuzmin repeats Ivan Susanin’s feat and prevents sabotage behind Soviet lines.

    Ordered to guide an enemy unit behind the Red Army troops who have broken through, Kuzmin obeys. All night, the old man leads the Germans through a blizzard across trackless terrain, but they have no idea what he is planning.

  11. 11

    Special Festival Award

    For an Outstanding Festival Debut

    Keep My Shadow

    Russia

    Director: Igor Zhuravlev

    About the Film

    A documentary journey through Joseph Brodsky’s Saint Petersburg. Archival Leningrad meets the city of today, while the poet’s verse and voice bring the space of memory to life.

    The streets, courtyards and windows reveal traces of a man who has long been absent. The camera seeks his invisible presence in a city that became part of his destiny. A reflection on time, separation and what cannot be erased from memory.

  12. 12

    Special Festival Award

    For Creating a Vivid National Character and Boldly Exploring the Deep Bonds Uniting Different Cultures

    Snow Flower

    Russia / India · co-production

    Awarded to the film’s actors:

    Chhaya Kadam — Nanda

    Vaibhav Mangale — Digya

    About the Film

    A Marathi-language Indian-Russian drama directed by Gajendra Ahire in close collaboration with Russian filmmakers. The story explores the emotional and cultural contrast between India’s tropical Konkan region and snow-covered Siberia.

Belarusian film director and screenwriter, founder of the production company Avantdrive. Director of the acclaimed films Spice Boys and Welcome to the Family. Recipient of the Best Feature Film Award at the New Season Online Cinema Festival.

International journalist and Chief Editor at the TASS Analytical Center. He specializes in international affairs, analysis, and coverage of global political and social processes.

Main structure of the regulations. The full festival regulations are available as a PDF document.

    Feature-length, short, documentary and popular science films from BRICS+ countries, as well as international co-productions, are eligible for participation.

    English subtitles are mandatory. Films in languages other than Russian must include both Russian and English subtitles.
